My working theory is that one has access to \[somewhat\] unlimited beans, but they are **unusable** unless cooked.

## 11 Undercooking Beans
undercooking beans can lead to you feeling overwhelmed or overstimulated
Examples:
Going to work without enough sleep
Sleep is a method of preparing beans ahead of time!
Being rushed or pressured into going somewhere
can lead to being overwhelmed or overstimulated
Going to work without enough sleep

- Sleeping is a form of bean cooking\!

Being rushed/pressured into going somewhere and being overwhelmed/overstimulated

This can lead to being overwhelmed or overstimulated


## 12 You Can Overcook Beans
Laying around all day thinking “I’ll clean this evening” but then it’s 10pm and you haven’t done anything
By not setting an end time, the beans go bad!!!
Laying in bed all day thinking “I’m gonna clean *later*”

- By not setting an end time to cooking your beans, you overcook them and they aren’t good
